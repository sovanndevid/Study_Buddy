## 📘 Project Brief

This project is a **Childcare Data Entry Form** created for *Little Zareef's Childcare* as part of a web development course. The form is designed to collect essential information about children enrolled in the childcare service, including their name, date of birth, medical conditions, and emergency contact details. It also captures additional instructions from parents or guardians. The form is accessible, uses semantic HTML5 elements, responsive CSS for styling, and basic client-side validation using HTML attributes. It follows best practices in frontend development, ensuring usability across devices and screen readers.

## 💡 Hypothesis

The form was designed based on the assumption that parents or guardians enrolling their children in a childcare service require a quick and intuitive method to provide critical information. By simplifying the interface and highlighting important input areas like emergency contact and medical conditions, the form helps ensure accuracy and completeness of data for staff members. The hypothesis is that a well-structured, accessible form improves the efficiency of administrative processing while reducing the chances of missing vital child-specific instructions.
